# CCNA Reviewer domain

The CCNA Reviewer is a personal study system for the Cisco CCNA v1.1 objectives. It connects a source-grounded learning roadmap with practical lab evidence and a private record of the learner's study activity.

## Curriculum language

**Objective**:
One of the 53 parent objectives in the Cisco CCNA v1.1 blueprint. Child items remain part of the objective's teaching and evidence requirements.
_Avoid_: Topic when referring to the exam blueprint item

**Lesson understanding**:
The learner's reading and conceptual progress for an objective, separate from quiz performance and lab demonstration.
_Avoid_: Exam readiness

**Lab demonstration**:
Evidence that the learner completed the practical requirements for a lab, including the expected configuration, verification, and allowed/forbidden behavior checks.
_Avoid_: Lab completion when evidence has not been identified

**Evidence mode**:
The trust level attached to lab evidence: `self_reported` when the learner records their own result, or `verified` when the result has been checked against a declared platform and evidence contract.
_Avoid_: Automatic verification unless a real checker exists

**Objective coverage**:
The number and percentage of the 53 objectives whose lesson-understanding requirement is complete. It is reported separately from labs, quizzes, study time, and streak.
_Avoid_: Blended progress score

## Activity language

**Study session**:
A manually recorded period of study with a calendar date, duration, and optional objective, lab, or note. A day counts toward the streak only when its recorded total reaches 30 minutes.
_Avoid_: Timer session

**Quiz attempt**:
One submitted run through a static multiple-choice quiz, including its score, selected answers, objective tags, and timestamp.
_Avoid_: Mastery unless a defined threshold is being discussed

**Streak**:
The number of consecutive local calendar days ending at the current study date that each contain at least 30 minutes of recorded study.
_Avoid_: Login streak

**Lab evidence note**:
The learner's short record of the topology, command output, test result, or limitation that supports a lab demonstration. It is not an uploaded artifact.
_Avoid_: Screenshot requirement

## Access language

**Owner**:
The single allowlisted learner account that uses the first release.
_Avoid_: Tenant, team, administrator

**Allowlisted email**:
The normalized email address admitted to the owner's account. Email/password and Google are the only supported login methods.
_Avoid_: Open registration
