# Keep learning evidence as separate dimensions

**Status:** accepted

The reviewer reports lesson understanding, lab demonstration, and quiz performance separately rather than compressing them into one progress score. This preserves the curriculum's distinction between conceptual learning, practical evidence, and assessment results, and prevents a completed reading item from being mistaken for a verified lab skill.

## Consequences

- The dashboard presents separate objective, lab, quiz, study-time, and streak metrics.
- Lab records carry a self-reported or verified evidence mode.
- A future readiness score can be added later without changing the meaning of existing records.
